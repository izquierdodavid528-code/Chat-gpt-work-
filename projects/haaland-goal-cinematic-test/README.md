# Haaland Goal Cinematic Test

A short stylized 3D football cinematic inspired by Erling Haaland's visual identity and power-finisher archetype.

This is **not a recreation of a specific real match or historical goal**. It is a fictional showcase scene designed to test the quality ceiling of our Blender pipeline.

## Sequence

1. Low-angle stadium reveal
2. Striker approaches the ball
3. Left-foot strike
4. Ball bends toward the top corner
5. Goal net reacts
6. Hero finish with number 9 silhouette

## Output

- 720x1280 vertical
- 24 fps
- 144 frames / 6 seconds
- Blender EEVEE
- MP4 + poster PNG + source .blend
