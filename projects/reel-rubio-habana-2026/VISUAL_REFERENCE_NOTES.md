# VISUAL REFERENCE NOTES — Rubio / La Habana 2026

Purpose: guide modeling, framing and material realism. Reference imagery is not automatically cleared for publication.

## Florida Straits
Reference goals:
- Florida occupies the upper/north portion of the frame;
- Cuba runs horizontally below it;
- the Straits remain the dominant negative space;
- Bahamas should not be implied as Cuba or Florida.

Useful reference types:
- satellite imagery of Florida Straits;
- nautical/geographic maps;
- official/open geodata for coastlines.

Production rule:
Use geographic data or manually traced public/open references for the final Blender coastline, not a decorative invented island silhouette.

## Oil tanker
Reference goals:
- aerial/three-quarter tanker silhouette;
- correct long deck proportions;
- piping and superstructure concentrated aft/midship depending on generic class;
- wake and ocean lighting physically plausible.

Do not:
- model a named real tanker unless the story is specifically about that vessel;
- imply a documented shipment that is not sourced.

## Havana
Reference goals:
- recognize the coastal character of the Malecón / Havana skyline;
- use real architectural proportions and coastal lighting;
- avoid generic “tropical city” substitutes.

Potential source pools:
- Wikimedia Commons for reusable reference/possible cleared media;
- official Cuban/municipal imagery where permissions are clear;
- licensed news footage only if rights permit.

## Marco Rubio
Use authentic official still/video material only when identity matters.
Preferred:
- U.S. Department of State official media;
- official government photography;
- licensed press material where available.

Never:
- synthetic documentary-looking footage;
- face replacement/deepfake;
- cloned speech presented as Rubio.

## Documents
Use real EO / OFAC document text as the factual source.
In Blender/Remotion:
- show dates, titles and a few key clauses;
- do not fabricate seals, signatures or quoted language;
- keep long legal text unreadable unless actually needed.

## Color/material direction
- ocean: deep blue/black, physically plausible specular response;
- land: graphite with subtle cool edge light;
- policy pressure: red emission used sparingly;
- documents: warm ivory / muted gold;
- institutional graphics: steel blue / warm white.

## Licensing rule
Reference ≠ permission to republish.
Every external still/video used in the final master needs a recorded source and reuse basis in the project asset log.
