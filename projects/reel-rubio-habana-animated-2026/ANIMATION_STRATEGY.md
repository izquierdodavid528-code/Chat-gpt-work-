# ANIMATION STRATEGY — Rubio / La Habana 2026

## Direction
Original editorial explainer; no slide reel and no imitation of a named creator. Use maps, acting, physical metaphors, transformations, continuity, callbacks and motivated camera to explain facts.

## Master rule
FLOW FABRICATES SELECTED SHOTS AND ELEMENTS. REMOTION DIRECTS AND COMPOSES THE FILM. BLENDER SOLVES SPECIFIC REMAINING PROBLEMS.

Flow is a first-class engine for both image and video; it may replace selected manual or Blender work when quality and continuity hold. Remotion handles edit, multicore layers, masks, crops, maps/routes, exact labels, dates, match cuts, parallax, captions, narration, SFX and music. Blender is reserved for named shots requiring repeatable geometry, perspective, rig, camera or lighting/depth Flow and Remotion cannot deliver efficiently.

Route each shot R (Remotion), FI (Flow image), FV (Flow video), B (Blender) or S (sourced media). Do not use a tool merely to show integration.

## Order
Source-check and time voice; build full voice-led 60-second animatic with existing Flow assets and deliberate placeholders; review complete motion at phone size; generate only missing shots; compose/refine in Remotion; use Blender only for a specific unmet need; finish voice/SFX/music/captions and separate QA gates.

No final vector-only remake. No chain of unrelated Flow clips. No generic wipes hiding discontinuity. Avoid document repetition: use water, routes, gates, calendar, sector tokens, stamp, bank/network, meeting/education symbols and timeline callbacks.
