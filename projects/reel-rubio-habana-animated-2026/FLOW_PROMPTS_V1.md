## Before generating

1. Select image-to-video or a new image generation. Attach the approved still image as reference/ingredient for a new scene.
2. Do not attach one of the existing MP4s if the goal is a separate new shot: the user's Flow interface edits an attached video.
3. Select portrait 9:16 and 6 seconds for the two planned video tests. Use the highest available output quality.
4. Keep generated text, dates, maps and logos out of the shot. Add exact details later in Remotion.
5. Make one candidate, inspect identity and action at phone size, then correct only a specific failure. Flow credits are available; the goal is to spend them on useful shots rather than duplicate near-identical paper clips.
