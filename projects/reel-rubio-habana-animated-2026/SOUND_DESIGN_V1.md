# SOUND DESIGN V1 — Illustrated Rubio / La Habana 2026

Goal: reinforce the animated explainer language without making the political subject feel sensationalized.

## Mix hierarchy

1. narration
2. action SFX
3. music bed

Narration must always remain the clearest element.

## Music

Direction:
- light documentary pulse;
- restrained percussion / plucked texture;
- no trailer impacts;
- no ominous war-drone treatment;
- no triumphant political tone.

Music should provide continuity while scene-specific SFX explain physical actions.

## SFX cue map

### 00:00–00:04 — hook
- soft map/paper whoosh on camera settle;
- four small object pops, each slightly different;
- very light ocean movement underneath.

### 00:04–00:12 — oil / customs
- low ship motor / water movement;
- cargo-box lift thunk;
- short travel whoosh as cargo crosses the frame;
- customs barrier: mechanical clack;
- January stamp: dry rubber-stamp hit.

### 00:12–00:18 — February
- calendar page flip;
- small reverse/swipe as tariff marker is removed;

### 00:18–00:29 — May toolbox

- lid/paper hinge;
- five sector tokens pop out with ascending pitch variation;
- State/Treasury arms: two cloth/slide whooshes;
- central implementation stamp: heavier but still clean;
- Rubio illustration: subtle character pop, not a comic boing.

### 00:29–00:41 — designations
- paper roll / receipt movement;
- OFAC stamp impacts at each animated designation;
- final bank transformation: paper fold + masonry/building settle;
- camera push into bank: short deep whoosh.

### 00:41–00:52 — finance + mobility
- coin/token metallic tick moving through network;
- pipe flow: soft filtered movement;
- U-turn cancellation: marker swipe / sign snap;
- route closed: gate thunk;
- professional badge tab: velcro/rip sound;

### 00:52–01:00 — close
- camera pullback whoosh;
- two speech bubbles: soft paper pops;

- final title: no impact; use only a gentle music resolve.

## SFX style rules

- one main SFX per visible action;
- do not layer impacts just to make a moment feel more dramatic;
- avoid gunshot-like stamp sounds;
- avoid sirens;
- avoid military foley unless the animation literally depicts the relevant object;
- keep most SFX short and dry so they read on phone speakers.

## Narration

Target pace: ~145–150 wpm.

Delivery:
- explanatory;
- conversational;
- neutral;
- no announcer voice;
- slight pauses around date changes so the animation can breathe.

## Captions

Do not create word-by-word karaoke subtitles.

Use phrase-level captions:
- 2 lines maximum;
- ~5–9 words per phrase;
- centered in lower safe area;
- captions should not cover active illustrated objects;
- move caption block upward during scenes with bottom action.
