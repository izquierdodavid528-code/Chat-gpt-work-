# STORYBOARD V1 — 60 s illustrated explainer

## 00:00–00:04 — Hook: layers arrive

Camera dives from a hand-drawn Caribbean map into the Florida Straits.
Four small illustrated objects enter around Cuba, one after another:
1. oil barrel;
2. official paper/stamp;
3. bank token;
4. conference badge / graduation cap.

No list on screen. The objects themselves establish the idea that pressure comes through different mechanisms.

Minimal text: "2026".

## 00:04–00:12 — January: oil -> trade barrier

An illustrated tanker sails toward Cuba carrying oil.
The camera follows the oil route.
Match cut: a cargo box from the same supplier heads toward a U.S. customs gate.
On "29 ENE", a red-white customs arm drops across the route.
Small tag: "EO 14380".

Meaning: the order created a mechanism allowing additional tariffs on imports from countries supplying oil to Cuba.

## 00:12–00:18 — February: the barrier changes

A calendar flips from JAN to 20 FEB.
A large animated eraser / rewind arrow removes the tariff mark and lifts the customs arm.
The underlying emergency paper remains pinned in the background.

Small tag: "EO 14389".

Meaning: the IEEPA tariff actions ended; do not visually imply the whole Cuba policy disappeared.

## 00:18–00:29 — May: one document becomes a toolbox

The pinned paper folds open and becomes a toolbox.
Illustrated sector icons spring out:
- oil derrick;
- mine cart / metal;
- bank;
- shield/security;
- defense crate.

Two cartoon hands/arms labeled STATE and TREASURY receive stamps from the document.
Rubio is introduced only in State's lane, as Secretary of State, not signer of the presidential order.

Small date: "1 MAY · EO 14404".

## 00:29–00:41 — June to September: designations accumulate

A roll of paper moves like a receipt/list.
OFAC stamp hits illustrated entries as the calendar advances.
June: ICAP / MINFAR examples.
September: an illustrated bank building appears; stamp lands on "BANCO EXTERIOR".

The bank building then becomes the transition into the finance scene.

## 00:41–00:52 — Finance + mobility

Camera enters the bank building.
Money/token travels through a network of pipes.
A U.S. bank junction changes from a U-turn arrow to a reject gate.
Beside it, a conference badge loses its green authorization tab and an education icon narrows into a smaller permitted lane.

Keep category-specific scope; do not imply a blanket travel ban.

## 00:52–01:00 — Close: two official framings, one chronology

The camera pulls back to the whole illustrated policy machine assembled over the island/map.
Two speech bubbles appear from opposite sides:
- WHITE HOUSE;
- MINREX.

They contain only a few attributed words, not paragraphs.
Then both shrink away.
Final image is the mechanism itself with the timeline Jan -> Feb -> May -> Jun/Sep.

Final factual idea:
During 2026 the architecture of restrictions changed and broadened in several areas.

## Transition rule

No scene starts from a blank screen.
Every scene grows out of the previous scene's last object.
