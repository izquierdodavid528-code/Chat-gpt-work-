# STORYBOARD V2 — 60-second voice-led explainer

Status: production plan; timing is provisional until the scratch narration is recorded. This replaces STORYBOARD_V1 for the next animatic.

## Art direction and action rule

Original illustrated editorial-cartoon / cut-paper world. Use the visual language the user likes—clear maps, acting objects, physical transformations, brisk rhythm and callbacks—without copying Memorias de Pez artwork, layout or branding. One visual action should explain each narration beat. Avoid static cards and a chain of unrelated AI clips. Put exact dates, maps, policy text and attribution in Remotion.

## Timed acts

### 00:00–00:04 — Hook: the policy machine wakes up
A camera move lands on a simplified, fact-checked Caribbean map. The existing harbor Flow clip supplies water/ship motion. In Remotion, four illustrated objects enter in rhythm: oil barrel, paper, bank token, meeting/education symbol. Keep the frame readable; do not label the objects as a list.

### 00:04–00:12 — 29 January: oil route / possible tariff
Track one tanker/oil route toward Cuba. Match-cut the moving cargo shape into an import box approaching a U.S. customs barrier. The barrier drops only when the date and tariff mechanism are named. Add 29 JAN / EO 14380 in Remotion. Do not imply that every supplier was automatically tariffed.

### 00:12–00:18 — 20 February: that tariff route ends
A calendar turns to 20 FEB. Remove the tariff marker and lift that customs barrier while leaving the broader policy map and other objects in view. Add EO 14389 in Remotion. This is a change in one mechanism, not a reset of all Cuba policy.

### 00:18–00:29 — 1 May: expanded sanctions / assigned roles
An official sheet unfolds into a physical toolbox; sector tokens emerge one at a time. Use the planned Flow acting clip for Rubio's deliberate look and point, then composite verified labels/icons in Remotion. Identify Rubio as Secretary of State, not as signer of the presidential order. Add 1 MAY / EO 14404 in Remotion.

### 00:29–00:41 — Designations through September
An unlettered register scrolls; a red stamp lands on a few entries. It folds/morphs into an illustrated bank exterior. Remotion adds the accurate OFAC / BANCO EXTERIOR DE CUBA labels and dates. No generated text or fake bank branding.

### 00:41–00:52 — Financial and travel rules
Camera enters a layered bank/network scene. A money token takes a U-turn route and meets a rule gate; a conference authorization marker is removed; an education route narrows but does not disappear. Use precise Remotion graphics and captions to preserve each rule's scope, including the limited wind-down period. This is not a blanket travel ban.

### 00:52–01:00 — Attributed close
Pull back to the map and assembled timeline. Show two clearly attributed framings (White House and MINREX) without implying they are equivalent factual claims. End on the policy timeline and a concise neutral summary. Build captions and wording from the final recorded voice, not from a guessed timing.

## Transition and motion beats

Every scene must grow from a prop, route, camera movement or shape in the previous scene. Use motivated match cuts and hand-drawn reveals; do not rely on identical wipes between unrelated full-frame shots. Across the minute, plan a readable event about every 0.7–1.5 seconds, with quieter holds for dates and voice pauses.

## Routing

- Flow: approved illustrated stills and selected acting/transformation/environment motion plates.
- Remotion: master edit, map geometry, dates, exact labels, policy diagrams, typography, overlays, subtitles, narration and audio mix.
- Blender: only named shots needing reliable 3D depth, perspective, lighting, rig/camera choreography or reusable 3D motion. Render short assets, not the entire reel.
