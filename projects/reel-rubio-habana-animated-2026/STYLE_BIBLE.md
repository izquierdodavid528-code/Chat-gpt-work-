# STYLE BIBLE — Rubio / La Habana 2026

Use rubio-character-master-v1.jpg as primary identity anchor. This is an original editorial illustration style; borrow only broad explainer clarity, never distinctive drawings, palette, layout, branding or assets from another channel.

## Rubio character lock
- Middle-aged illustrated man; light-medium skin; oval/rectangular face, broad chin, dark swept side-part hair, dark brows, large expressive eyes, straight nose.
- Slight caricature in head/torso, adult full-body proportions, hands/shoes readable. Stable character scale by shot type.
- Deep navy suit, white shirt, coral/salmon tie, small lapel flag pin when present, brown shoes. No wardrobe, age, hairline or tie-color changes.
- Expression is restrained and legible through gaze/brow/mouth; avoid permanent anger or exaggerated mockery.
- Hands must read as simple natural gestures with clear start/hold/end. Reject fused or extra digits, clipping through props and unstable poses.
- Never regenerate identity from text alone when approved still reference is available.

## Visual treatment
- Hand-inked editorial cartoon, clean filled shapes and subtle tactile paper grain; layered 2D/2.5D depth, not photorealism or generic corporate icon art.
- Medium-heavy charcoal outline, organic but consistent; sparse interior detail.
- Observed anchor palette: warm ivory, muted navy, coral-red, sea blue, restrained olive/mustard, charcoal. It is not a requirement to repeat every color in every shot.
- Soft warm key and short contact shadows; no neon, harsh war-room light or ominous treatment.
- Clear foreground/midground/background. Parallax is restrained and does not replace acting.
- Harbor, map, tabletop, bank or clean warm backgrounds only when the action needs them.
- One motivated camera move and one primary action per Flow shot; define start and end state.

## Mobile layout
Vertical 9:16 master, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Keep face/prop/labels in central 80%; reserve lower safe area for 1–2 caption lines. Rubio medium framing generally 45–65% frame height when acting; full-body only if environment/action matters.
All exact names, dates, EO numbers, legal wording and maps are Remotion layers, never generated text.

## Continuity / negatives
Preserve face, hairline, lapels, shirt, tie knot/color, body scale, line weight, light and screen direction. Use prior approved end frames as references when supported. Do not repeat documents as universal transition: use wakes, routes, gates, calendar edges, toolbox hinges, stamp circles, facades, network nodes, badge straps, timeline callbacks.
Exclude photorealism, identity redesign, wardrobe drift, extra politicians, malformed hands, fake text/logos/maps/legal documents, watermark/UI, blanket flags, generic wipes, paper-only montage, propaganda staging and imitation of a specific creator.

## Acceptance
Compare at phone size to the anchor; check face/outfit, line/texture, palette, hand anatomy, framing, focal clarity and motion continuity. After one targeted correction, reroute a repeatedly failing shot to Remotion or (only for a concrete depth/geometry need) Blender.
